# Brag plan — SupposeVeterinary

## Angle
A Kathmandu neighborhood pet supply shop and veterinary clinic in one place. Show the real storefront, then follow one product from its listing into the WhatsApp order drawer. Finish on the clinic's published 24/7 emergency line.

## Hook
“Shop + clinic. Under one roof.” This is the site's own promise, shown over the real home page.

## Highlights
- Real product listings with photographs, categories, prices and stock labels.
- Add a listed product to the order drawer and send the list through WhatsApp; the site states that online payment is not needed.
- The published emergency line says the clinic answers 24/7.

## Punchline / close
SupposeVeterinary — “Caring for your pets like family.” Kathmandu, Nepal, with the clinic phone number visible.

## Tone and visual identity
Polished and neighborly, with brisk but calm motion. Use the project's emerald green, warm white interface, rounded cards and system sans-serif. The product UI is captured from the locally running project with its actual catalog data and component behavior. Music is an original, soft G-major synth piece with blended pluck and click accents.
